    Your old basic insurance only ends once the new insurer confirms you are covered. Nothing lapses in between.

    About this page

    What it is

    A free, independent tool that compares the official 2027 basic health insurance premiums in all 26 Swiss cantons and works out what a year would cost for the answers you give. It is not affiliated with any health insurer or with the Federal Office of Public Health, earns no commissions and does not arrange insurance. Insurer names and trademarks belong to their owners.

    Known limits

    • All 26 cantons, for ages 19 to 25 and 26+. Children are not included. The page is in English, with German terms.
    • Yearly cost uses the legal adult rules for deductible and the 10% co-pay (capped at CHF 700). The CHF 15/day hospital contribution is left out.
    • Matching what you pay to a plan allows for the CO2 levy refund of about CHF 5 a month on 2026 bills. Discounts or special policies can stop a match.
    • Whether a model "suits you" rests on three simple questions. Every insurer's model has its own detailed rules.
    • Deadlines: changing insurer or lowering a deductible must reach your insurer by 30 November. Other changes can differ by insurer.
    • Perks come from insurers' public pages and cover 13 insurers. Some documents are from 2025 or earlier.

    How yearly cost is worked out: 12 months of premium, plus your bills up to the deductible, plus 10% of the rest up to CHF 700 a year (legal adult rules). The CHF 15/day hospital contribution is left out because it is the same everywhere. Bills on 2026 statements are reduced by the CO2 levy refund, about CHF 5 a month, which is why your bill can be a little lower than the listed premium.

    How the suggestions work: fixed rules on the official prices. A switch saving under CHF 100 a year is shown as a tiny saving, CHF 100 to 300 as a small saving. Models you said would not suit you are never recommended. Year-on-year change compares the same model at the same deductible; renamed or new models show "new".

    Perks: collected in October 2026 from each insurer's own pages, linked beside every entry. Some documents are from 2025 or earlier and insurers change extras every year, so confirm amounts before relying on them. Smaller insurers are not covered yet.
